WORKPLACE AND LABOUR RIGHTS IN INDIA - KEY PROVISIONS

APPLICABLE LAWS:
- Industrial Disputes Act, 1947
- Payment of Wages Act, 1936
- Minimum Wages Act, 1948
- Payment of Bonus Act, 1965
- Employees' Provident Fund Act, 1952
- Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972
- Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013
- Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020

KEY EMPLOYEE RIGHTS:

1. RIGHT TO MINIMUM WAGES:
- Every employer must pay at least the minimum wage fixed by Central/State Government
- Current Central minimum wage: Rs. 178/day (unskilled) to Rs. 868/day (highly skilled) - varies by state
- Non-payment is a criminal offence
- Complaint: Labour Commissioner office

2. RIGHT TO TIMELY PAYMENT:
- Wages must be paid before 7th of month (establishments with <1000 workers)
- Before 10th of month (establishments with >1000 workers)
- No unauthorized deductions allowed
- Deductions cannot exceed 50% of wages

3. RIGHT TO PROVIDENT FUND (EPF):
- Applicable to establishments with 20+ employees
- Employer must contribute 12% of basic salary
- Employee also contributes 12%
- Cannot be denied if eligible
- Check balance: epfindia.gov.in or UMANG app

4. RIGHT TO GRATUITY:
- After 5 years of continuous service
- Amount: 15 days wages for each year of service
- Maximum: Rs. 20 lakh
- Payable on retirement, resignation, death, or disability

5. RIGHT AGAINST WRONGFUL TERMINATION:
- Cannot be terminated without notice (1-3 months as per appointment terms)
- Retrenchment requires 1 month notice + compensation (15 days avg pay per year of service)
- Establishments with 100+ workers need government permission for retrenchment
- Termination during maternity leave is illegal

6. RIGHT TO SAFE WORKPLACE:
- Employer must provide safe working conditions
- Proper ventilation, lighting, sanitation
- Safety equipment for hazardous work
- First aid facilities
- Working hours: Maximum 48 hours/week, 8 hours/day (with overtime pay for excess)

7. RIGHT AGAINST SEXUAL HARASSMENT:
- Every workplace must have Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)
- Women can file complaint within 3 months of incident
- Employer must conduct inquiry within 90 days
- Complaint: ICC or Local Complaints Committee
- Protection against retaliation

8. MATERNITY BENEFITS:
- 26 weeks paid leave for first two children
- 12 weeks for third child onwards
- Cannot be terminated during maternity
- Right to work from home (if nature of work permits)
- Creche facility mandatory for 50+ employees

9. RIGHT TO BONUS:
- Employees drawing up to Rs. 21,000/month are eligible
- Minimum bonus: 8.33% of salary
- Maximum bonus: 20% of salary
- Payable within 8 months of closing of accounting year

WHAT TO DO IF RIGHTS ARE VIOLATED:

Step 1: Document everything (emails, letters, pay slips, attendance records)
Step 2: Raise internal grievance with HR/management in writing
Step 3: If unresolved, approach Labour Commissioner/Labour Inspector
Step 4: File complaint with appropriate authority:
- Wages issues: Labour Commissioner
- PF issues: EPFO (epfigms.gov.in)
- Sexual harassment: ICC or Local Complaints Committee
- Wrongful termination: Labour Court/Industrial Tribunal
- Safety violations: Factory Inspector/Occupational Safety Officer

COMPLAINT TO LABOUR COMMISSIONER FORMAT:

To,
The Labour Commissioner,
[District/State]

Subject: Complaint regarding [non-payment of wages/PF/wrongful termination etc.]

Respected Sir/Madam,

I, [Name], was employed at [Company Name], [Address] as [Designation] from [Start Date] to [End Date/continuing].

I wish to bring to your notice the following violation of labour laws:
1. [Describe violation in detail]
2. [Second issue if any]

Despite repeated requests to the management, no action has been taken. I request your kind intervention.

Documents enclosed:
- Appointment letter
- Salary slips
- [Other relevant documents]

Yours faithfully,
[Name]
[Address]
[Phone]
[Date]
