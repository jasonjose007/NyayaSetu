MAJOR GOVERNMENT SCHEMES AND WELFARE PROGRAMS - INDIA

1. PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
Category: Agriculture/Farmers
Benefit: Rs. 6,000 per year in 3 installments of Rs. 2,000 each
Eligibility: All landholding farmer families (subject to exclusions)
Exclusions: Income tax payers, government employees, professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs)
How to Apply: Visit nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or apply online at pmkisan.gov.in
Documents: Aadhaar, land records, bank account details

2. PM AWAS YOJANA (PMAY) - Housing for All
Category: Housing
Benefit: Financial assistance for house construction/purchase
- Urban: Rs. 2.5 lakh (EWS/LIG), interest subsidy up to Rs. 2.67 lakh (MIG)
- Rural (PMAY-G): Rs. 1.20 lakh (plain areas), Rs. 1.30 lakh (hilly areas)
Eligibility: Family should not own a pucca house, annual income criteria applies
How to Apply: Through local municipal body (urban) or Gram Panchayat (rural), or online at pmaymis.gov.in
Documents: Aadhaar, income certificate, proof of no ownership of pucca house

3. AYUSHMAN BHARAT (PM-JAY) - Health Insurance
Category: Health
Benefit: Rs. 5 lakh per family per year for hospitalization
Eligibility: Families identified in SECC 2011 data (rural) and occupational criteria (urban)
- Auto-included categories: Manual scavengers, primitive tribal groups, legally released bonded laborers
How to Apply: Visit nearest Ayushman Bharat centre or empanelled hospital with Aadhaar
Documents: Aadhaar, ration card, any government ID
Verification: Check eligibility at mera.pmjay.gov.in or call 14555

4. MGNREGA (Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act)
Category: Employment/Rural
Benefit: 100 days of guaranteed wage employment per household per year
Eligibility: Any rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work
How to Apply: Apply to Gram Panchayat for Job Card
Documents: Proof of residence, photographs, Aadhaar
Important: If work not provided within 15 days of application, unemployment allowance is payable

5. PM MUDRA YOJANA - Business Loans
Category: Entrepreneurship/Business
Benefit: Collateral-free loans up to Rs. 10 lakh
- Shishu: Up to Rs. 50,000
- Kishore: Rs. 50,001 to Rs. 5 lakh
- Tarun: Rs. 5,00,001 to Rs. 10 lakh
Eligibility: Any Indian citizen with a business plan for non-farm income generating activity
How to Apply: Approach any bank, NBFC, or MFI
Documents: Business plan, identity proof, address proof, caste certificate (if applicable)

6. SUKANYA SAMRIDDHI YOJANA - Girl Child Savings
Category: Women/Children
Benefit: High interest savings account (currently ~8%) with tax benefits
Eligibility: Girl child below 10 years, maximum 2 accounts per family
How to Apply: Any post office or authorized bank
Documents: Birth certificate of girl child, parent's ID and address proof
Minimum deposit: Rs. 250/year, Maximum: Rs. 1.5 lakh/year

7. PM VISHWAKARMA - Traditional Artisans
Category: Artisans/Craftsmen
Benefit: Recognition, skill training, toolkit, credit support up to Rs. 3 lakh, market access
Eligibility: Traditional artisans and craftspeople working with hands in 18 identified trades
Trades: Carpenter, blacksmith, goldsmith, potter, sculptor, cobbler, tailor, basket/mat maker, etc.
How to Apply: Register at pmvishwakarma.gov.in through CSC
Documents: Aadhaar, skill/trade proof

8. NATIONAL PENSION SCHEME (NPS) / ATAL PENSION YOJANA (APY)
Category: Pension/Social Security
Benefit (APY): Guaranteed pension of Rs. 1,000-5,000/month after age 60
Eligibility (APY): Indian citizens aged 18-40, with bank account
How to Apply: Through bank or post office
Government co-contribution: 50% of premium (up to Rs. 1,000/year) for 5 years for eligible subscribers

9. STAND-UP INDIA - SC/ST/Women Entrepreneurs
Category: Entrepreneurship
Benefit: Bank loans between Rs. 10 lakh to Rs. 1 crore for greenfield enterprise
Eligibility: SC/ST and/or Women entrepreneurs, above 18 years
How to Apply: Through standupmitra.in portal or directly at bank branch
Documents: Business plan, identity proof, caste certificate, project report

10. PM SURAKSHA BIMA YOJANA - Accident Insurance
Category: Insurance
Benefit: Rs. 2 lakh for accidental death/full disability, Rs. 1 lakh for partial disability
Premium: Rs. 20 per year (auto-debit from bank account)
Eligibility: Bank account holders aged 18-70 years
How to Apply: Through bank (auto-debit consent form)

11. PM JEEVAN JYOTI BIMA YOJANA - Life Insurance
Category: Insurance
Benefit: Rs. 2 lakh to nominee in case of death
Premium: Rs. 436 per year (auto-debit)
Eligibility: Bank account holders aged 18-55 years
How to Apply: Through bank

12. NATIONAL SCHOLARSHIP PORTAL
Category: Education
Multiple scholarships for:
- Pre-matric and Post-matric students from SC/ST/OBC/Minority communities
- Merit-based scholarships
- Means-cum-merit scholarships
How to Apply: scholarships.gov.in
Documents: Income certificate, caste certificate, previous year marksheet, bank details, Aadhaar

ELIGIBILITY CHECKER CRITERIA:
When determining scheme eligibility, consider:
- Annual family income
- Caste category (General/SC/ST/OBC)
- Residence (Rural/Urban)
- Age and gender
- Occupation
- Land ownership
- BPL/APL status
- Bank account status
- Aadhaar linking
- State of residence (some schemes are state-specific)
