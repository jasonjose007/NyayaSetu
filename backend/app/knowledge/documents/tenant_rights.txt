TENANT RIGHTS IN INDIA - KEY PROVISIONS

SOURCES OF LAW:
- Model Tenancy Act, 2021 (Central Government model for states to adopt)
- State-specific Rent Control Acts (vary by state)
- Transfer of Property Act, 1882 (Section 105-117)
- Indian Contract Act, 1872

KEY TENANT RIGHTS:

1. RIGHT TO WRITTEN AGREEMENT:
- Every tenancy must be in writing (Model Tenancy Act, 2021)
- Agreement must specify rent, duration, terms of renewal, and conditions
- Both landlord and tenant must sign

2. RIGHT TO RECEIPT:
- Tenant has right to receive receipt for every rent payment
- If landlord refuses to accept rent, tenant can deposit in court

3. RIGHT AGAINST ARBITRARY EVICTION:
- Landlord cannot evict without valid grounds
- Valid grounds include: non-payment of rent for 2+ months, subletting without permission, misuse of property, landlord's personal need
- Proper notice must be given (usually 1-3 months depending on state)
- Only court/tribunal can order eviction

4. RIGHT TO ESSENTIAL SERVICES:
- Landlord cannot cut water, electricity, or other essential services
- Disconnection of services to force eviction is illegal
- Tenant can file complaint with Rent Authority

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT:
- Model Tenancy Act limits: maximum 2 months rent for residential
- Deposit must be returned within 1 month of vacancy (after deducting damages if any)
- Landlord cannot make arbitrary deductions
- If landlord refuses to return, tenant can file complaint

6. RIGHT TO PRIVACY:
- Landlord must give 24 hours notice before entering premises
- Cannot enter without tenant's permission except in emergency
- Repeated unauthorized entry is harassment

7. RIGHT AGAINST DISCRIMINATION:
- Cannot refuse tenancy based on religion, caste, gender, marital status, food habits
- Some state laws specifically prohibit such discrimination

8. RENT INCREASE:
- Cannot increase rent during tenancy period without agreement
- Model Tenancy Act: rent revision not before 12 months
- Increase must be as per agreement terms or reasonable

WHAT TO DO IF LANDLORD REFUSES TO RETURN SECURITY DEPOSIT:

Step 1: Send a written notice (email/registered post) demanding return within 15 days
Step 2: Document the condition of property at time of vacating (photos/videos)
Step 3: If not returned, send a legal notice through advocate
Step 4: File complaint with Rent Authority/Tribunal (under Model Tenancy Act)
Step 5: Alternative: File case in Civil Court or Consumer Forum

[Landlord Address]

Under instructions from my client [Tenant Name], I serve upon you this Legal Notice:

5. Your failure to return the deposit is in violation of the rental agreement and applicable tenancy laws.

You are hereby called upon to refund the entire security deposit of Rs. [Amount] within 15 days of receipt of this notice, failing which my client shall be constrained to initiate appropriate legal proceedings against you, at your risk, cost, and consequences.

COMMON TENANT DISPUTES AND REMEDIES:
- Illegal eviction: File FIR + approach Rent Tribunal
- Services cut off: File complaint with Rent Authority + police
- Deposit not returned: Legal notice + Consumer Forum/Civil Court
- Excessive rent increase: Challenge before Rent Authority
- Maintenance issues: Written notice to landlord, if ignored approach authorities
- Harassment: Document everything, file police complaint if criminal

IMPORTANT CONTACTS:
- Rent Authority/Tribunal (varies by city)
