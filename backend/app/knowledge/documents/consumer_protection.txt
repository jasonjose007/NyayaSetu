CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT, 2019 - KEY PROVISIONS

DEFINITION OF CONSUMER (Section 2(7)):
Any person who buys goods or avails services for consideration. Includes offline and online transactions. Does NOT include a person who obtains goods for resale or commercial purposes.

CONSUMER RIGHTS (Section 2(9)):
1. Right to be protected against marketing of hazardous goods and services
2. Right to be informed about quality, quantity, potency, purity, standard and price
3. Right to be assured access to variety of goods and services at competitive prices
4. Right to be heard and assured that consumer interests will receive due consideration
5. Right to seek redressal against unfair trade practices or restrictive trade practices
6. Right to consumer awareness

UNFAIR TRADE PRACTICES (Section 2(47)):
- False representation about quality, standard, grade, composition, style or model
- False claims about sponsorship, approval, affiliation
- Making false or misleading claims about the need for goods or services
- Selling goods not complying with standards
- Misleading advertisements
- Not issuing bill or receipt for goods sold or services rendered above prescribed value

PRODUCT LIABILITY (Section 82-87):
A product manufacturer, seller or service provider is liable for any harm caused by defective product or deficient service. Complainant need not prove negligence or fault.

WHERE TO FILE COMPLAINT:
1. District Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum - claims up to Rs. 1 crore
2. State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission - claims Rs. 1 crore to Rs. 10 crore
3. National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission - claims above Rs. 10 crore

HOW TO FILE A CONSUMER COMPLAINT:
1. Write a complaint with your name, address, and details of opposite party
2. Describe the goods/services purchased with date and amount
3. State the defect in goods or deficiency in service
4. State the relief sought (refund, replacement, compensation)
5. Attach supporting documents (bills, receipts, warranty cards, correspondence)
6. Pay the prescribed fee based on claim amount
7. File within 2 years from the date of cause of action

COMPLAINT FORMAT:
Before the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum at [City]

CONSUMER COMPLAINT

Complainant: [Your Name and Address]
Opposite Party: [Seller/Service Provider Name and Address]

FACTS OF THE CASE:
1. The complainant purchased [product/service] from the opposite party on [date] for Rs. [amount].
2. [Describe the defect/deficiency in detail]
3. The complainant brought this to the notice of the opposite party on [date] but no action was taken.

GROUNDS:
- The product/service is defective/deficient as per Section [relevant section]
- This amounts to unfair trade practice under Section 2(47)
- The opposite party has failed to provide satisfactory remedy

RELIEF SOUGHT:
1. Refund of Rs. [amount] paid for the defective product/deficient service
2. Compensation of Rs. [amount] for mental agony and harassment
3. Cost of litigation
4. Any other relief deemed fit

VERIFICATION:
I hereby verify that the contents of this complaint are true and correct to my knowledge.

[Date]
[Place]
[Signature]

ONLINE FILING:
Complaints can also be filed online at: edaakhil.nic.in (e-Daakhil portal)
National Consumer Helpline: 1800-11-4000 or 1915

IMPORTANT TIMELINES:
- File within 2 years of cause of action
- Forum must decide within 3 months (5 months if testing required)
- Appeal within 45 days of order
- Fee: Rs. 100 to Rs. 5,000 based on claim amount

REMEDIES AVAILABLE:
- Removal of defect from goods
- Replacement of goods
- Refund of price paid
- Compensation for loss or injury
- Discontinuation of unfair/restrictive trade practice
- Adequate costs to parties
